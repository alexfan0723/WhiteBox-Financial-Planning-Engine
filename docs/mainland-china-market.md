# Mainland China Market Orientation

WhiteBox is designed from the mainland China financial planning context.

This matters because household planning assumptions are market-specific:

- family responsibility structures differ from overseas templates
- housing can dominate household balance sheets
- insurance products and advice workflows have local characteristics
- retirement planning must consider local income, social security, and family support patterns
- education funding and intergenerational transfers are often central planning topics
- liquidity and property-market constraints require local treatment

## Expert Co-Design

WhiteBox is designed with input from local practitioners, including financial planning and insurance professionals.

The goal is not to copy a foreign planning model and translate labels. The goal is to build a locally grounded planning system with explicit assumptions, auditable calculations, and advisor-reviewable outputs.

## Public Research Position

Public examples in this repository should use sanitized, fictional, non-customer data.

When examples involve market assumptions, they should state:

- currency and units
- jurisdiction
- time horizon
- household structure
- income and expense interpretation
- asset liquidity assumptions
- insurance treatment assumptions
- whether the output is research, preview, or formal
